# 🤖 AI Impact on Students’ Performance

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an integral part of modern education. From **exam preparation** and **note-taking** to **doubt-solving** and **tutoring**, students are increasingly relying on AI-powered tools.  
This project explores **how AI usage impacts students’ academic performance**.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains detailed information about students’ demographics, study habits, AI usage behavior, and academic performance indicators.

- **Demographics:** Age, Gender, Grade Level  
- **Study Habits:** Study hours, sleep hours, tutoring hours, study consistency  
- **AI Usage:** AI tools used, usage time, purpose, dependency score  
- **Performance Metrics:** Concept understanding, final score, pass/fail status  
- **Target Variable:** Performance Category (Low / Medium / High)  

---

## 🔍 Key Questions Explored

### 🤖 AI Usage & Student Performance
- Do AI users perform better than non-AI users?  
- Do AI users pass more often than non-AI users?  
- Does AI usage influence students’ performance categories?  

### 🧠 AI Dependency & Conceptual Understanding
- Does higher AI dependency affect students’ conceptual understanding?  
- Does better concept understanding lead to higher final scores?  

### 🎯 Purpose & Tool of AI Usage
- Does the purpose of AI usage affect student performance?  
- Does the purpose of AI usage influence conceptual understanding?  
- Does the choice of AI tool influence student performance?  

### 📚 Study Habits & Learning Behavior
- Do more daily study hours lead to higher final scores?  
- Does study consistency lead to improvement in student performance?  

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- **Weak correlation between study hours and final scores** (r = 0.02) – quality of study matters more than quantity.  
- High AI dependency can negatively impact **conceptual understanding**, even if short-term scores improve.  
- The **purpose and type of AI tool** significantly influence learning outcomes.  
- Balanced study habits combined with responsible AI usage lead to **better performance**.
